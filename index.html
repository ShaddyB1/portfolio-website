Hey, thanks for stopping by.

I'm Shadrack Boateng Addo. I grew up between Ghana and Canada.

I'm studying Computer Science (with a minor in Economics) at the University of Victoria, and I work as a Data Analyst and BI Developer. I like turning messy operational data into something a team can actually use on Monday morning.

At Karrio I automated reporting pipelines, tuned SQL across SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and Redshift, and shipped Tableau / Power BI / SSRS dashboards for Finance and Ops. That work cut a lot of manual spreadsheet grinding, roughly 65% less reporting overhead for the workflows I owned.

Outside dashboards I also build small products when a problem needs a front door: a citizenship countdown, a football league site, a hospital finder for Ghana. Same instinct either way: clarify the decision, then ship the view.

Fun fact

I will happily debug a warehouse query at midnight, but I still double-check IRCC day counts on paper first.
